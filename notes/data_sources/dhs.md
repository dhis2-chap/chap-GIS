# DHS (Demographic and Health Surveys)

Geolocated household survey clusters with malaria outcomes.

- **Variables:** Household cluster locations, malaria RDT results, individual-level prevalence
- **Coverage:** Rwanda (2014–15, 2019–20), Tanzania (2015–16)
- **Access:** Pre-downloaded processed GeoJSON/CSV under `data/malaria-outcomes/dhs*/processed/`; original data requires DHS Program approval
- **Used in malaria-research:** `scripts/dhs_cluster_exposure.py`; cluster-level exposure validation
