# WHO World Malaria Report 2024 — Annexes

Annual WHO malaria reporting in spreadsheet form.

- **Variables:** Cases and deaths (estimated and reported), species breakdown, ITN distribution, funding
- **Format:** Excel spreadsheets
- **Coverage:** Global
- **Access:** HTTP download from `https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/malaria/world-malaria-reports`
- **Used in malaria-research:** `scripts/download_who_malaria.py`; epidemiological analysis
