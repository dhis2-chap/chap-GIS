# CHIRPS

Climate Hazards Group InfraRed Precipitation with Station data.

- **Variables:** Monthly rainfall totals
- **Resolution:** ~5 km
- **Coverage:** Africa (full product is global)
- **Access:** HTTP download from `https://data.chc.ucsb.edu/products/CHIRPS-2.0/africa_monthly/tifs`
- **Used in malaria-research:** `scripts/uganda_enhanced_regression.py`; precipitation covariate extraction
