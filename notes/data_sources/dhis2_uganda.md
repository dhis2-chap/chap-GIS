# DHIS2 — Uganda HMIS

Routine health management information system data from Uganda.

- **Variables:** Confirmed malaria cases by district, monthly (2021–2025)
- **Coverage:** Uganda
- **Access:** Pre-downloaded CSV/GeoJSON under `data/`; sourced from the Uganda DHIS2 instance
- **Used in malaria-research:** `scripts/uganda_district_exposure_correlation.py`; district-level case data
