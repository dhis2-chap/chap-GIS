# Malaria Atlas Project (MAP)

Modelled malaria epidemiological surfaces and survey points.

- **Variables:** *P. falciparum* parasite rate (PfPR), *P. falciparum* incidence rate, malaria survey points
- **Resolution:** ~1 km (raster products); points for surveys
- **Coverage:** Global, extracted by country
- **Access:** WCS 2.0.1 and WFS via GeoServer at `https://data.malariaatlas.org/geoserver`
- **Used in malaria-research:** `malaria_research/data/map.py`, `scripts/download_map.py`; epidemiological validation
