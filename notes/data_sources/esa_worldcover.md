# ESA WorldCover

Global land-cover classification.

- **Variables:** 11-class land cover (2021)
- **Resolution:** 10 m
- **Coverage:** Global (tile-based); used for Rwanda, Tanzania, Uganda
- **Access:** AWS S3 bucket `https://esa-worldcover.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/v200/2021/map`
- **Used in malaria-research:** `malaria_research/data/worldcover.py`; breeding-site identification (wetlands, water bodies)
